Setup

Arrange your fleet

Drag a ship from the dock or your board. You can also select a ship below, then click or tap its starting square. Use Rotate to change direction. Keyboard: arrow keys on your board, Enter/Space to place, R to rotate.

Both boards are concealed. Pass the device before continuing. The other player should look away while you arrange your fleet or take your turn.

Field guide

Sink all five opposing ships before your fleet is sunk. One shot per turn, even after a hit.

Keyboard: Tab to enemy waters, use arrow keys to navigate, then Enter or Space to fire. Home / End moves to the row edges. Fired squares remain available for inspection.
